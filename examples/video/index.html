NVIDIA VSR, running locally

This 640×360 motion test plays in Helium. The extension sends its video frames to NVIDIA's VSR engine on your RTX GPU and draws the enhanced result over the source. Playback controls remain with the original player; VSR follows the audio output clock.

Open the extension toolbar button and choose Enable enhancement, or use VSR OFF in the page controls. Hold Original to compare. If the panel is missing, launch this page with ./vsr browser.

Frames pass through browser memory. Its output adds processing latency and uses GPU AV1 compression. The AI processing itself uses NVIDIA VideoSuperRes; no replacement upscaler is used.

Try Big Buck Bunny on YouTube · Choose a source resolution, select a target in the extension, then enable VSR.
